Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Dawn is an iPhone app that displays your Google Search Console data. This policy explains what Dawn accesses, where it is kept, and what it does not do.

The short version

  • Dawn has no server. The developer never receives your Search Console data, your Google account details or your sign-in tokens.
  • Dawn's access to your Google account is read-only and limited to Search Console.
  • Dawn contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

What Dawn accesses

Google Search Console data

When you sign in with Google, Dawn requests a single permission: read-only access to Search Console data for your verified sites (the webmasters.readonly scope). With it, Dawn reads the list of your sites and their search performance data: clicks, impressions, click-through rate, average position, search queries and page URLs.

Dawn cannot change anything in your Search Console account, and it does not request access to your email, contacts, profile or any other Google service.

Sign-in tokens

Google issues tokens that let Dawn fetch your data. They are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and are sent only to Google.

How your data is used and where it is kept

Search Console data is used only to show you the screens, widgets and morning report notification inside Dawn. It is requested directly from Google by the app on your device and cached on your device so the app and its widgets can open quickly. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party, it is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

The morning report is a local notification created on your device; its content is not sent anywhere.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Dawn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Purchases

Dawn Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Dawn learns only whether a subscription is active on your Apple account; it does not receive your payment details.

Sample data

The "Explore with sample data" mode uses built-in example data. It does not use a Google account and does not send or store any personal data.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Signing out in Dawn's Settings removes the sign-in tokens from your device.
  • Deleting the app removes the data Dawn cached on your device.
  • You can revoke Dawn's access at any time in your Google account under Third-party apps and services.

Children

Dawn is a tool for website owners and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
