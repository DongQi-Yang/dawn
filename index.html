Dawn

Your Search Console, every morning. Ten seconds to know what moved yesterday.

TodayClicks, impressions, CTR and position for the latest day, with the changes worth a look.
TrendsSee how your site has moved over time, compared with the period before.
ExploreYour top queries and pages, each with its own daily curve.
Read-only and on your phoneDawn asks Google for read-only access to your Search Console data. There is no Dawn server: your data goes from Google straight to your iPhone and stays there.

Dawn Pro

Dawn is free for one site. Dawn Pro is an optional subscription that adds all of your sites, home screen widgets and a morning report notification.
